From Lathes to Lambda Functions

For 100 years, the name Schindler has been synonymous with precision engineering. My grandfather built physical machines—gears, shafts, and pistons that powered the industrial boom. He believed that if you build it right, it lasts forever.

Today, Schindler Machine Works operates in a new dimension. The materials have changed from cast iron to cloud infrastructure, from grease to git repositories. But the philosophy remains unchanged.

We don't just "write code." We engineer digital machinery designed to withstand the load of modern enterprise.
