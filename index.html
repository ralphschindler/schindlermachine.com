Since 1923

From Lathes to Lambda Functions

For over a century, the name Schindler has been synonymous with precision engineering. The company was founded on the principle of building machines that last—gears, shafts, and pistons that powered the industrial boom. The belief was simple: if you build it right, it lasts forever.

Today, Schindler Machine Works operates in a new dimension. The materials have changed from cast iron to cloud infrastructure, from grease to git repositories. But the philosophy remains unchanged.

We engineer digital machinery designed to withstand the load of modern enterprise.
